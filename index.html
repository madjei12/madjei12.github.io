Bio

A Graduate Assistant at Dr. Catherine Nakalembe's XylemLab, currently pursuing an M.S. in Geospatial Information Science at the University of Maryland, College Park. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Geography and Resource Development from the University of Ghana, Legon, with hands-on experience in GIS and remote sensing tools such as ArcGIS Pro and QGIS. I regularly work with programming languages and platforms like Python (using IDEs such as Spyder and VS Code), as well as SQL, PHP, HTML and CSS to support spatial analysis, web mapping, and database management.

Outside the tech sphere, I also wear a creative hat — I am a freelance writer and spoken word artist. I look forward to collaborating in both fields, so feel free to reach out and connect anytime!
